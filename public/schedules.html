This creates a default schedule of 1-hour slots from 08:00 to 22:00 (last start 21:00) for 5 days per week, repeated for the next 4 weeks for this room.

This uses the default working hours. Specific days can be overridden below using exceptions and blackout dates.

Exceptions (Per Room & Date)

Use this to override the base schedule for a specific room + date. Orange slots are unavailable; click to turn them green (available).

For this date, only the green slots are bookable. Orange slots become unavailable for that day and room.

Blackout Dates (Per Room)

Select a room and a date to fully disable that room for that entire day. All slots become unavailable and any bookings on that day are auto-cancelled.
